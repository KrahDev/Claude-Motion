# Discovery Interview — 8 Questions Before Any Frame

Generic videos are rarely a motion problem. They are a **briefing** problem: the
model fills every unknown with the statistical average — three sentences per
scene, a cut every second, a purple glow, "Unlock your potential". The average
IS the slop. These eight questions remove the unknowns that cause it.

## How to run the interview

- **Always ask all 8 questions**, for every new video — work, private, or
  anything else. Also for "quick" requests. A 5-second logo sting still needs
  a purpose, an audience, and a pace.
- **Ask in the user's language.** The canonical wording below is German with an
  English version; translate faithfully for any other language.
- **Pre-fill what the request already answers.** If the prompt says "30s Reel
  for my bakery", don't ask blank — propose: *"Ich gehe von Instagram-Reel, 30 s,
  Ton aus im Feed aus — stimmt das?"* The user confirms or corrects. Never
  silently skip a question because you think you know the answer.
- **Offer options, not essays.** Every question has 2–4 concrete options plus
  free text. In Claude Code use the `AskUserQuestion` tool (max 4 questions per
  call → two calls: Q1–Q4, then Q5–Q8). In chat, send one numbered message;
  the user may answer in shorthand ("1b, 2 Instagram, 3 …").
- **One follow-up round, max.** Only for answers that are vague or
  contradictory (see "Vague answers" below). Then build.
- **Opt-out only on explicit request.** If the user says "keine Fragen, mach
  einfach" / "skip the questions", use the safe defaults at the bottom, write
  them into the brief as *assumptions*, and list them in your delivery message.
- **Editing an existing video/project:** ask all 8 too, but phrase them as
  "what's wrong with the current version?" for Q5–Q8 — that's where the fix is.

## The 8 questions

### Q1 — Anlass & Ziel / Occasion & goal
> **DE:** Wofür ist das Video, und was soll die Person danach **tun, fühlen oder
> wissen**? (z. B. beruflich: Produkt vorstellen, Stelle besetzen, Kunden
> gewinnen · privat: Geburtstag, Hochzeit, Erinnerung · sonstiges: Verein,
> Portfolio, Kampagne)
>
> **EN:** What is the video for, and what should the viewer **do, feel, or
> know** afterwards?

Options: *Handeln* (klicken, kaufen, bewerben, anrufen) · *Fühlen* (gerührt,
begeistert, stolz, lachen) · *Verstehen* (Erklärung, Ablauf, Zahl) ·
*Wiedererkennen* (Marke, Logo, Stimmung)

Why: decides the ending. "Handeln" → CTA scene with one action. "Fühlen" → no
CTA, the payoff is an emotional hold. A birthday video with a "Jetzt
entdecken"-CTA is slop.

### Q2 — Publikum & Abspielort / Audience & playback
> **DE:** Wer schaut das Video, **wo und wie**? Mit Ton oder stumm? Wie lang
> darf es sein?
>
> **EN:** Who watches it, **where and how**? Sound on or muted? How long may it be?

Options: *Social Feed* (Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn, oft stumm, 9:16 oder 4:5) ·
*Präsentation/Beamer* (16:9, Ton da, Publikum sitzt) · *Privat verschickt*
(WhatsApp/Familie, Handy, Ton meist an) · *Loop* (Messe, Website-Hero,
Bildschirm im Schaufenster — stumm, endlos)

Why: sets format, duration, safe zones, and whether meaning may depend on
audio. Muted playback + voice-over-only message = invisible message.

### Q3 — Die eine Botschaft & die Streichliste / One message & cut list
> **DE:** Wenn sich der Zuschauer an **nur einen Satz** erinnert — welcher ist
> es? Und: Was willst du alles zeigen? Markiere davon die **max. 3 Punkte,
> die unverzichtbar sind** — der Rest fliegt raus.
>
> **EN:** If the viewer remembers **one sentence only**, which one? List
> everything you'd like to show, then mark the **max. 3 that are essential** —
> everything else gets cut.

Why: information overload is the #1 cause of unwatchable videos. One scene =
one idea. The unmarked items are NOT "squeezed in somewhere" — they are cut.
If the user insists on more than 3, propose a longer video or a series,
never denser scenes.

### Q4 — Das Unverwechselbare / The unmistakable detail
> **DE:** Was gibt es **nur bei dir / euch / diesem Anlass**? Eine echte Zahl,
> ein Name, ein Insider, ein Ort, ein Gegenstand, ein Zitat, ein Foto — etwas,
> das in keinem anderen Video vorkommen könnte.
>
> **EN:** What exists **only for you / this occasion**? A real number, a name,
> an inside joke, a place, an object, a quote, a photo — something that could
> appear in no other video.

Why: specificity is the opposite of slop. "Seit 1987 in dritter Generation",
"Omas Kuchenrezept", "412 gelöste Tickets im März" beats any generic claim.
This detail becomes the hero of the PAYOFF scene. If the user has none, ask
once more with examples; if still none, the video must stay short and
honest rather than padded with invented claims.

### Q5 — Text & Sprache im Bild / On-screen text & voice
> **DE:** Wie viel **Text** darf im Video stehen, und gibt es **Sprache**
> (Voice-over, O-Ton)? Du oder Sie?
>
> **EN:** How much **text** may appear on screen, and is there **speech**
> (voice-over, original audio)? Formal or informal address?

Options:
- **A — Kein Text**, nur Bild/Logo (Bild erzählt alles)
- **B — Schlagworte**: max. 3–5 Wörter pro Szene
- **C — Kurze Sätze**: max. 1 Satz (≤ 10 Wörter) pro Szene
- **D — Untertitel** zu Sprache/Voice-over

Why: "too many sentences" is the most common complaint about AI videos. The
chosen option becomes a hard cap (see translation table). Text the viewer
cannot finish reading is noise.

### Q6 — Tempo, Schnitt & Ton / Pace, cutting & audio
> **DE:** Welches **Tempo** passt? Und wie soll es **klingen** (Musik, Effekte,
> Stille)?
>
> **EN:** What **pace** fits? And how should it **sound** (music, SFX, silence)?

Options:
- **Ruhig** — lange Einstellungen, weiche Übergänge (Hochzeit, Erinnerung,
  Premium-Marke, Behörde/Institution)
- **Ausgewogen** — klarer Rhythmus, Raum zum Lesen (Erklärvideo, Recruiting)
- **Energisch** — schnelle Schnitte auf den Beat (Launch, Sport, Event-Teaser)

Audio: *Musik + Effekte* · *Nur dezente Musik* · *Nur Effekte* · *Stumm*
(plus: vorhandene Musik/Voice-over-Datei?)

Why: "cuts too fast" is the second most common complaint. Fast is not
automatically modern — fast + text = unreadable.

### Q7 — Look, Marke & echtes Material / Look, brand & real material
> **DE:** Gibt es **Logo, Farben, Schriften, Fotos, Videos, Screenshots**, die
> rein sollen? Wenn nicht: beschreibe den Look in **3 Adjektiven**.
>
> **EN:** Are there **logo, colors, fonts, photos, footage, screenshots** to
> use? If not: describe the look in **3 adjectives**.

Why: real material (the actual product, the actual people, the actual place)
always beats generated abstract shapes. Brand colors replace the default
palette — never put the default purple on someone's brand. Ask where the
files are and copy them to `public/`.

### Q8 — No-Gos / Anti-references
> **DE:** Was hast du in anderen Videos gesehen, das du **auf keinen Fall**
> willst? Gibt es ein Video, das dir gefällt — und eins, das du schlimm
> findest?
>
> **EN:** What have you seen in other videos that you **definitely don't
> want**? Is there a video you like — and one you find awful?

Offer a checklist (multi-select): *Neon/Glow* · *Glitch-Effekte* ·
*Schreibmaschinen-Text* · *Emojis* · *Konfetti/Partikel* · *Wackelnde
Kamera/Zoom-Gewitter* · *Stock-Fotos mit lächelnden Menschen* ·
*Werbe-Floskeln* ("revolutionär", "next level", "Game Changer") ·
*Filmkorn/Vignette* · *Zu dunkel*

Why: users describe what they hate far more precisely than what they want.
Every checked item goes into the brief's NO-GO list and is enforced at
verification.

## Vague answers → one targeted follow-up

| Vague answer | Follow-up |
|---|---|
| "modern", "professionell", "cool" | "Eher wie Apple (ruhig, viel Weißraum) oder wie Red Bull (laut, schnell)?" — always a contrast pair |
| "einfach schön" | "Hell oder dunkel? Warm oder kühl? Verspielt oder elegant?" |
| "so viel wie möglich zeigen" | Re-ask Q3: "Welche 3 davon würdest du behalten, wenn nur 3 gingen?" |
| "egal" on pace/text | Use the safe default AND tell the user which one you picked |
| Contradiction (e.g. "ruhig" + 15 Infos in 15 s) | Name the conflict and let the user choose: "Ruhig oder vollständig — beides in 15 s geht nicht." |

## Translation table — answers → hard production constraints

These numbers go into `brief.md` and `theme.ts` (`PACE` = `calm` | `balanced`
| `energetic` for Ruhig | Ausgewogen | Energisch, and `MAX_WORDS`). They are
**caps and floors**, enforced during build and verification.

### On-screen text (Q5)

| Option | Max words / screen | Max text blocks / scene |
|---|---|---|
| A — Kein Text | 0 (logo/name only, final scene) | 0 |
| B — Schlagworte | 5 | 1 |
| C — Kurze Sätze | 10 | 1 (+ optional 1 small label) |
| D — Untertitel | 7 per caption chunk | 1 caption line + max 1 keyword |

**Reading-time floor** (all options): a text block stays fully visible for at
least `fps * (1.0 + words / 3)` frames — 1 s orientation + ~3 words/s. At
30 fps: 3 words → 60 frames, 9 words → 120 frames. If the pace doesn't allow
the floor, **cut words, never time.**

### Pace (Q6)

| Pace | Min shot length | Typical shot | Transition | Holds | Springs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ruhig | 3.0 s | 4–6 s | 15–24 frames, crossfade/slide | 30+ frames | `smooth` only; no `bouncy` |
| Ausgewogen | 1.5 s | 2–4 s | 8–14 frames | 15–20 frames | `smooth` + `snappy` |
| Energisch | 0.5 s | 0.8–1.5 s | 0–6 frames, hard cuts on beat | 8–12 frames | `snappy` + `bouncy` accents |

Text floor beats pace: an energetic video with text uses short words, not
short reading time.

### Information density (Q3)
- Number of BODY beats = number of essential points (1–3). Never more.
- One idea per scene. If a scene needs "and", split it or cut it.
- Total duration ≈ hook + (beats × typical shot length × 1–2) + payoff + end.
  If the user's requested length is shorter than the content needs, say so
  before building.

### Ending (Q1)
- Handeln → CTA scene, ONE action, ≤ 5 words, calm, glow on the action word.
- Fühlen → no CTA. End on the unmistakable detail (Q4) + a long hold
  (≥ 1.5 s) + soft fade.
- Verstehen → end on the result/number, then logo.
- Wiedererkennen → end on logo/brand mark, 1–2 s breathing hold.

### Playback (Q2)
- Muted playback (feed, loop) → the message must work **without sound**: text
  or visuals carry it; voice-over gets burned-in captions.
- Loop → last frame must flow into the first (same background, no CTA hard stop).
- Präsentation/Beamer → 16:9, larger type (≥ 120 px hero at 1920), no
  9:16 safe-zone rules, but keep 5 % edge margin.

### No-gos (Q8) vs. the 10 rules
No-gos **override stylistic defaults** of this skill: if the user rejects
grain, vignette, glow, mesh backgrounds, or bouncy springs, drop them (keep a
subtle non-flat background via a gentle gradient instead). No-gos **never
override craft rules**: easing, clamping, fps-derived timing, one theme
object, and the render → inspect → fix loop stay mandatory.

## Anti-slop language rules (apply to every word on screen)
- No filler or marketing clichés: *revolutionär, next level, Game Changer,
  innovativ, nahtlos, unlock, elevate, seamless, journey, "Willkommen in der
  Zukunft"*. Replace with the concrete fact from Q4.
- No invented numbers, quotes, testimonials, or awards. Only what the user
  gave you. Missing fact → ask, or leave it out.
- No placeholder text (*Lorem ipsum, Your Brand, Company Name*) in a render
  shown to the user.
- Write like the user speaks (Du/Sie from Q5, their own words from Q1/Q3 when
  possible). Show the user every on-screen text in the brief **before**
  building — text changes after rendering are the most expensive fix.

## `brief.md` — write this to the project root before any code

```markdown
# Creative Brief — <Projektname>
Kontext: <beruflich | privat | sonstiges> — <Anlass>
Ziel (Q1): <Handeln | Fühlen | Verstehen | Wiedererkennen> — <konkret>
Publikum & Abspielort (Q2): <wer> · <Plattform> · <Ton an/stumm> · <Format> · <Dauer>
Kernbotschaft (Q3): "<ein Satz>"
Beats (Q3): 1. … 2. … 3. …   Gestrichen: …
Unverwechselbares Detail (Q4): <…>  → Hero im PAYOFF
Text (Q5): Option <A–D> → max <n> Wörter/Screen · Voice-over: <ja/nein> · <Du/Sie>
Tempo & Audio (Q6): <Ruhig|Ausgewogen|Energisch> → min. Einstellung <x> s · Audio: <…>
Look (Q7): <Assets mit Pfaden> · Farben <…> · Schrift <…> · Adjektive <…>
NO-GOs (Q8): <Liste>
Annahmen (falls Fragen übersprungen): <…>

## Szenenplan
| # | Zeit | Idee (eine!) | Text im Bild (exakt) | Wörter | Dauer ≥ Lesezeit? |
|---|------|--------------|----------------------|--------|-------------------|
```

Show the brief (at least Kernbotschaft, Szenenplan with exact texts, NO-GOs)
to the user and get a "passt" before Step 2. One confirmation round, not a
negotiation.

## Safe defaults (only when the user explicitly skips the interview)
Q1 Verstehen · Q2 Social Feed, stumm, 9:16, 15 s · Q3 1 Botschaft, 2 Beats ·
Q4 none (keep it short, no invented facts) · Q5 B Schlagworte ·
Q6 Ausgewogen, dezente Musik + Effekte · Q7 neutral light palette, no brand
colors guessed · Q8 Emojis, Werbe-Floskeln, Glitch.
